# Formal final research audit

Valid claims concern conditional predictive outputs and explicitly defined held-out metrics for the exact dataset, universe, horizon, fold, source versions and evaluation cutoff. Proper scores (CRPS, NLL where a density exists, Brier, pinball), PIT, intervals, ranking and calibration diagnostics characterize predictions under their stated assumptions. No forecasting engine guarantees trading profitability. Predictive metrics alone do not establish realized alpha, executable capacity, capital allocation or profits.

TRAIN fitting and learned gates/weights/normalization use admitted TRAIN evidence. VALIDATION evaluates a finite predeclared candidate pool; every attempt, including failure/incompatibility, remains in the opportunity denominator. TEST is sealed post-selection evaluation; recorded reuse/adaptivity does not erase selection bias. Bonferroni/Holm control requires valid constituent tests; BH assumptions differ from BY dependence protection. Sparse opportunities, adaptive research and undeclared searches limit interpretation. Model selection and ensemble construction remain separate operations.

Calibration supports native isolated methods with immutable fit states. Reliability estimates require support; conformal coverage depends on exchangeability, not arbitrary regime shifts. Quantile-only and sparse calibrated output do not establish a complete density. Gaussian/empirical/mixture laws preserve GT/GE/LT/LE ties and native expectation/variance instead of inventing a collapsed Gaussian. Horizons are independently fitted and scored; no joint law, horizon averaging or square-root-time inference is fabricated.

Uncertainty components can be unavailable and need not add to one variance. Identifiability assumptions distinguish parameter, aleatoric, expert disagreement and event/regime uncertainty; unavailable is not zero. Orthogonalization removes specified TRAIN linear redundancy, not all dependence or causality. Invariance/stability tests are descriptive rather than causal proof. Event/regime conditioning depends on observable evidence, support, timing and provider quality; nonstationarity can invalidate past relationships.

Transaction-cost evaluation is a hypothetical research scenario. Side/round-trip meanings, spread/fees/slippage/impact/turnover, missing and zero states and double-counting rules are explicit. Model-dependent impact is not executable capacity or a portfolio. Health thresholds are predeclared descriptive diagnostics; champion/challenger uses common matched support and MANUAL_ONLY advice, never automatic promotion/retraining/recalibration.

Historical reconstruction proves only its qualified delivery contract and local approval, not literal historical ingestion by this system. Source scalar authenticity remains external. Bounded numerical, synthetic statistical and offline service tests validate implementation behavior; they do not prove production reliability, market latency, broker quality or future economic value.
