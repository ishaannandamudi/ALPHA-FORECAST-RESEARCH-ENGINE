# Regime and event uncertainty

AFRE-11 soft memberships supply normalized probabilities. `−sum(p log p)` is exact discrete entropy in nats, not return variance. A present hard membership has zero entropy; fallback without membership is unavailable. Weighted state-mean/offset spread is exact arithmetic under supplied memberships and TRAIN fits, labelled `PROXY_ONLY` for uncertainty attribution. Missing active state means produce `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. A fitted mean is not itself a probability distribution over outcomes or model truth.

AFRE-10 conditioned-vs-base absolute shift is a descriptive `DISAGREEMENT` proxy. No event-state probabilities are supplied, so event variance is incompatible. Event class/subtype, regime kind/active states, source fallback, missing experts and sequence completeness are retained in conditional evaluation cohorts. Minimum counts control availability of cohort coverage/sharpness.

Event/regime forecast laws may supply exact total predictive variance where earlier phases preserve law shape and scale. No causal event attribution, invented state joint law, or automatic risk interpretation follows from the source law. Each target/horizon remains separate.

Predictive uncertainty does not establish trading profitability. These are descriptive forecast artifacts; portfolio construction, risk optimization and execution remain downstream projects after AFRE. AFRE-15 has no direct external-engine dependency.
