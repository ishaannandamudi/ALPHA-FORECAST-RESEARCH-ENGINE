# Point-in-time universe and ranking

The established [PIT universe specification](point-in-time-universes.md) defines
AFRE-3 membership, eligibility and immutable snapshots. AFRE-9 uses those exact
contracts for [same-origin cross-sectional ranking](cross-sectional-ranking.md).
Historical delisted constituents remain eligible in their historical snapshots;
current constituents and current classifications cannot be projected backward.
