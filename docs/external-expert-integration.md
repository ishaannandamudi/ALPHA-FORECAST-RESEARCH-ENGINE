# Neutral external expert integration

Downstream HFT integration supplies stable data artifacts from microstructure/AME, macro/context/MCE,
event/EIE and options specialists. AFRE imports none of their implementations or private classes,
reads no sibling databases, assumes no sibling paths and executes no provider-selected plugin.

ExternalExpertForecast pins its ExpertReference, stable security via full origin, exact target/horizon,
as-of, six-clock AvailabilityEvidence, SourceReference, quality and point or raw complete distribution.
Revision strings and exact content hashes produce append-only corrections. Provider model IDs are
opaque pinned artifact identities; AFRE validates structural hashes and provenance consistency,
without claiming cryptographic/provider authenticity or auditing how an opaque scalar was computed.

Keep upstream publication, upstream source availability, actual AFRE receipt, ingestion, computation
and final availability separate. A provider timestamp is not automatically AFRE availability.
Live receipt/processing floors are mandatory: publication10:00 and receipt10:02 is unusable at10:01.
An archive downloaded months later remains late even when its source says10:00. Qualified historical
delivery evidence may establish earlier historically knowable availability only when it binds the exact
source/security and a declared locally approved attestor. Actual later AFRE receipt/processing clocks
remain stored; reconstruction never pretends the system literally received it earlier.

Neutral expert outputs may be correlated with base/event/regime outputs. No independence, causal
importance, trading profitability, position size, capital allocation or execution decision follows
from a mixture weight or predictive metric. Portfolio construction, risk optimization and execution
remain downstream projects after AFRE.
