# Aleatoric and epistemic semantics

Aleatoric uncertainty concerns outcome variability conditional on known information. Epistemic uncertainty concerns knowledge of model, parameters or specification. **Aleatoric != epistemic.** A predictive law combines assumptions and evidence; its total variance alone does not identify those causal sources.

AFRE-15 estimates an OLS residual-noise quantity only under the explicit iid, homoskedastic, correct fixed linear design contract. This is partially identified and conditional on that model. Parameter prediction variance is a separate estimation quantity under the same assumptions. Neither is asserted to be a complete posterior uncertainty budget, nor added to a residual predictive distribution without an exact compatible identity.

Finite-mixture within variance is weighted component predictive variance. It is not automatically aleatoric. Between variance is the mathematical expert disagreement term. **Disagreement != epistemic automatically.** Different experts may share data, assumptions and bias; no independence or posterior-expert interpretation is assumed. Model specification spread is a declared alternative-model diagnostic, not a posterior over model truth.

Calibration measures reliability of probabilities or intervals. It does not identify why uncertainty exists. Sharp intervals may be badly calibrated. Data quality diagnoses provenance and missingness; **quality diagnostics != variance**. No risk budget, capital allocation, trade confidence or position size follows from these artifacts.

Predictive uncertainty does not establish trading profitability. These are descriptive forecast artifacts; portfolio construction, risk optimization and execution remain downstream projects after AFRE. AFRE-15 has no direct external-engine dependency.
