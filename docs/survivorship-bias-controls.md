# Survivorship-bias controls

AFRE-3 reconstructs membership from historical evidence, not today's constituent
list filtered by security age. CURRENT_CONSTITUENTS definitions are rejected.
Complete coverage is an explicit source guarantee; absence without it is UNKNOWN.
Knowledge and valid time are checked independently, so future inclusion announced
early and historical membership learned late cannot leak into earlier cutoffs.

A delisted security remains in the security master, prior membership, market
history and frozen snapshots. Eligibility changes only under legitimately known
and effective lifecycle/listing/tradability evidence. No current existence query
filters history. An acquisition does not rename historical rows to the acquirer;
parent and spinoff remain distinct. ADR/common/preferred and dual listings do not
collapse through shared issuers or display symbols.

The offline regression suite demonstrates A/B/C with C subsequently delisted,
future D inclusion, OLD→NEW on one stable security and ABC reused by another.
It covers venue changes, independent listings, delayed receipt, unqualified and
qualified archives, future prices/volumes, halts, history, currency, completeness
and ambiguity. Four hash seeds, reversed insertion, restart and concurrent/backdated
correction preserve old snapshots byte-for-byte. Rebuilding with later knowledge
creates a distinct identity and cannot serve an earlier causal origin.

PIT declarations still depend on truthful provider IDs, completeness claims,
clocks, calendars and reconstruction evidence. Hashes cannot authenticate them.
Eligibility is research sample selection and does not establish executable
liquidity, borrow availability, net trading returns or profitability. Portfolio
construction, risk optimization and execution remain downstream projects.
