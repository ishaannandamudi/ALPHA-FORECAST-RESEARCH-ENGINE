# Hypothesis families

A hypothesis pins the conditional-sign null, alternative, explicit GREATER/LESS/TWO_SIDED
direction, metric, effect definition, target/horizon, test method, assumptions, minimum
nonzero support and declaration clock. Direction, null, metric and target changes create
new IDs. Different metrics are separate opportunities, never pooled into one untyped test.

A family pins its exact planned attempt set, primary and optional additional correction
reports, explicit alpha/q, dependence assumption and declaration clock. All methods are
reported atomically; the engine offers no post-TEST method-selection argument. Family
membership and level are fixed before each native fold's training cutoff for claimed
predeclaration. Clocks cannot prove human preregistration or blindness; the attestor must
be honest about what outcomes were already inspected.

Each declared attempt consumes one slot in m, even repeated hypotheses, FAILED,
UNAVAILABLE, INSUFFICIENT or UNSUPPORTED evidence. Arithmetic uses p=1 internally for
missing/invalid raw evidence; published absent raw/adjusted values remain null. This
conservatively retains opportunity cost without inventing observed p-values.

COMPLETE means every declared attempt has an explicit completion, including unsuccessful
ones. OPEN/PARTIAL may expose explicitly interim adjusted values but no rejection.
INVALID is incompatible. Adaptive/unknown or retrospective families remain descriptive
and INTERIM even when all slots have completions. Final evidence still depends on valid
raw sampling assumptions; a completeness flag cannot establish independence or causality.

Actual1h/1d target, label, fold and source IDs remain distinct. The demo contains both,
two native AFRE17 environments and two AFRE18 cost scenarios in one declared family.
No automatic family construction, outcome-dependent removal, alpha optimization or
follow-up research is implemented. See [registry](research-trial-registry.md).
