# Research overfitting diagnostics

ResearchOverfittingDiagnostic counts all registered opportunities by hypothesis,
target/horizon, metric, native source, environment, cost scenario and inspected-choice
set. It reports completed/failed attempts, adaptive/unknown plans and repeated
TEST/dataset/fold/target/horizon/metric assessments. Uncompleted plans remain visible.

PREDECLARED is caller attestation with a checked declaration cutoff; ADAPTIVE requires
known parent trial identities available by declaration; UNKNOWN stays explicit. A later
trial declared after an earlier result is visibly adaptive. Neither timestamps nor
family hashes prove genuine human preregistration or reveal unregistered research.

Bonferroni/Holm/BH/BY address a declared multiplicity problem under valid assumptions.
They do not eliminate data snooping, leakage, selection opportunity, nonstationarity,
publication bias or adaptive hypothesis generation. P-value != effect size; significance
!= causality; significance != profitability. Exact conditional-sign inference assumes
independent fair nonzero signs; financial dependence often makes that assumption unsafe.
Unsupported assumptions return status rather than a manufactured significant result.

DeflatedPerformanceDiagnostic always reports UNSUPPORTED: AFRE has no portfolio-return
series or justified generic deflation contract. AFRE18 hypothetical arithmetic contribution
streams are not Sharpe-return samples. CI/SE and parametric normal/correlation inference
are also unsupported. No model selection, auto promotion, pruning or research loops.
Portfolio construction, risk optimization and execution remain downstream projects.

AFRE20 Hyperparameter & Model Selection Framework is deferred: explicit candidates/search
spaces; TRAIN/VALIDATION selection; deterministic search; no TEST tuning; candidate
registry; budgets; stable ties; criteria; model-family comparisons; nested selection when
needed. No ensemble fusion until AFRE21. See [controls](multiple-testing-controls.md).
