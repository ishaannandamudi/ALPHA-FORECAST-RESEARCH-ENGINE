# PIT sector and universe stability

Sector and industry environments use exact AFRE-9 `GroupMembership` evidence with stable security identity, taxonomy/provider/version, half-open valid time and final source availability. Ambiguous admissible classifications fail closed. Later reclassification remains an excluded later vintage and cannot rewrite an earlier membership.

Universe environments identify the exact native `UniverseDefinition` of the admitted AFRE-3 snapshot. Eligibility and included membership are validated for every sample. Historical universe identity and the complete forecast origin remain in lineage. A comparison of different security pools is a native-cohort descriptive comparison, not a claim that samples are identical or free of survivorship effects.

IC and ranks keep same-origin context distinct from temporal panels. Prediction comparisons require exact matched sample/full-origin/target identities. AFRE-9 higher-is-better average ties are retained for rank displacement; top K uses a declared K and stable sample-identity tie break for fixed-size selection. Cross-context or temporal comparisons retain prediction statistics but report rank diagnostics unavailable. Bucket-monotonicity extensions are deferred.
